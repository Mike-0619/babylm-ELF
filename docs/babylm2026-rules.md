## Tracks Relevant To This Project

The 2026 competition continues the two standard English-language tracks:

- `Strict`: train on a corpus of 100M words or less.
- `Strict-Small`: train on a corpus of 10M words or less.

The rules state that these tracks do not require the official BabyLM corpus. Participants may use the provided corpus or construct/swap in other data, as long as they stay inside the 100M or 10M word budget.

Models in these two tracks are evaluated on language-only evaluation tasks.

## Data And Training Limits

For the 10M and 100M tracks:

- The submission model's corpus budget is 10M words for `Strict-Small` and 100M words for `Strict`.
- The leaderboard training exposure limit counts repeated exposures:
  - `Strict-Small`: at most 100M words seen by the model.
  - `Strict`: at most 1B words seen by the model.
- The paper says this is usually equivalent to up to 10 epochs on the standard BabyLM corpora.
- The word exposure count includes input words and generated tokens seen by the submission model.
- Training on less than the upper bound is allowed.
- Compute itself is not directly restricted.

## Checkpoints

Intermediate checkpoints must be uploaded to Hugging Face Hub.

Required checkpoint schedule:

- Every 1M words until 10M words are seen.
- Every 10M words until 100M words are seen.
- For tracks other than `Strict-Small`, every 100M words until 1B words are seen.

## External Models, Multimodal Data, And Interaction

The former vision and interaction tracks are merged into `Strict` and `Strict-Small`.

Participants may use multimodal data or interactive training procedures, but they must obey the same `Strict` or `Strict-Small` data limits.

The rules distinguish:

- `submission model`: the model entered into the competition.
- `external model`: a secondary model used in the training pipeline but not submitted.

Important restrictions:

- External models must come from a predetermined list available on the BabyLM website.
- Text generated by external models counts toward the submission model's 100M or 10M word limit.
- The submission model may not generate more than 100M words for the external model in `Strict`, or 10M words in `Strict-Small`.
- If scalar rewards are generated by an external model for RL, the same 100M or 10M reward limit applies.
- Distillation is not allowed unless the external model's training word count is counted toward the submission model's limit.
- The external model's tokenizer, weights, hidden states, or output distribution cannot be revealed to the submission model.

## Dataset

The organizers provide updated detoxified datasets based on previous BabyLM corpora:

- 100M word `Strict` dataset.
- 10M word `Strict-Small` dataset.
- 100M word + image dataset.

The paper points to:

- OSF dataset link: https://osf.io/ad7qg/
- Hugging Face collection: https://huggingface.co/collections/BabyLM-community/babylm-2026

The provided datasets are a starting point. The rules allow swapping part or all of the training data with other sources, as long as the 10M/100M constraint is respected.

## Evaluation And Submission

Evaluation continues to use an open-source pipeline building on the 2025 challenge repository. Much of the evaluation is based on zero-shot probability comparisons between text sequences. Hidden tasks will be released shortly before the submission deadline.

Competition submissions are made through OpenReview and must include:

- A Hugging Face link for downloading the model and intermediate checkpoints.
- Model predictions in a format compatible with the evaluation pipeline and Hugging Face leaderboard.
- A datasheet and download link for any custom dataset, if not using a BabyLM-provided corpus.
- If using an external model in `Strict` or `Strict-Small`, the fine-tuning/distillation data for the external model, if any, and any data generated by the submission or external model.

Predictions must be uploaded to a Hugging Face leaderboard before the deadline to count as competition entries.

## Timeline From The CFP

The CFP labels the timeline as tentative:

- Mid February: CFP and training data released.
- May 25: ARR submission deadline.
- Mid July: direct submissions deadline.
- Early August: direct submission reviews due and ARR commitment deadline.
